Set your home and vehicle once in Settings, Travel, and every class with a campus room gets the travel buffer, past ones included. A ridden trip stops at the back gate or the front parking, whichever gives the shorter total, then walks in; the route planner starts at home and shows both legs. The vehicle picker has icons. The new plan button is a circle `+` in the sheet header beside close. Removes the Zendeck tab, spirits and streaks. Staging and prod serve OSRM, with steps to push map data.
