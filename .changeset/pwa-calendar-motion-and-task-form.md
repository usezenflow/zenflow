Calendar switches days and weeks with a slide, and views fade in. The day view is phone-only; desktop opens on week. The desktop week strip is a compact row. Session blocks draw travel zones that move with the session, and month drags show the chip above the grid. The task form shows its choices inline, with sliders for length and reminders and a custom reminder. A saved task's date and time can be moved. The desktop task sheet is narrower. Dates in the pickers follow the app language, and past days are disabled in the deadline picker.
