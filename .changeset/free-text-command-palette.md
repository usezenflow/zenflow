The home screen takes free text: type a task and tap the length, day or part of day it offers. Edits learn your usual changes and apply in one tap with undo, and one sheet picks a date and time without allowing the past. Recap shows insights, such as a day with most of the busy time, days without a lunch break and tasks with no time yet. Find time, replan and undo run from the same field, and view, move and delete pick a session from a list. Bulk create can offer a time choice when two placements differ by an hour. The palette's learned titles and places back up to your account.
