The app is now a thin planning layer over your phone calendar. Zenflow events sync to the device calendar with a 30 minute alert, and moving one on the phone teaches placement. Home, month and task screens give way to one range view, and setup, settings and the calendar sync are what remain. Onboarding connects campus, calendar and assistant on one screen. Tasks take an earliest start and a time window, are capped at 240 minutes and stay put once you move them. Assistants plan through MCP with a forced preview, undo and sync status, and are approved with a code shown in the browser. Reward learning scores kept sessions at 0.5 and no longer learns from deletes. Marks changes send a push, up to three discovery pushes go out, and iOS checks the inbox every 30 minutes for local notifications. Reminders, uploads, the campus map and the web app are gone. The Android icon matches iOS.
